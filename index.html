Privacy policy

BP Diary (Tansiyon Günlüğü) is an offline diary for blood pressure readings you type from a cuff. This phone does not measure blood pressure, and the app does not diagnose you.

Effective 30 September 2026. Package id com.presslog.bpdiary. English is the source text.

Data stays on your phone

Readings, notes, tags, and profiles are stored in a database on the device. They are not sent to us. There is no account and no cloud copy of the diary.

We do not collect, sell, or share that diary. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no advertising identifier.

  • Systolic, diastolic, pulse, the date and time, notes, and tags stay on the phone.
  • Profile names, language, category-color choice, and reminder times stay on the phone.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes this data unless you exported a copy.

Reminders

Morning and evening reminders are local notifications. Nothing is uploaded. The phone asks for notification permission only if you turn a reminder on. If you decline, the reminder stays off.

Health apps

Apple Health and Health Connect are not connected. The diary is not written to those services in this version.

Optional purchase

Settings offers an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. History, charts, PDF, and CSV stay open whether you pay or not. Apple or Google processes the payment. The app does not receive your card number, name, or email. A record that you bought it can stay on the device so Restore purchase works.

Export and import

PDF and CSV leave the phone only when you choose to share them. The file then goes to the app you pick, such as Mail or Files. We do not receive it. A CSV you import is added to the diary on this phone.

Deletion

You can delete a reading or a profile in the app. Because we never receive the diary, there is no server copy for us to erase. Uninstalling the app deletes what is still on the device.

Children

The app does not create an account and does not send diary contents to us. It is a log for readings a person already took, not a service that collects children’s information on a server.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. The diary on your phone is not uploaded in order to announce a change.
